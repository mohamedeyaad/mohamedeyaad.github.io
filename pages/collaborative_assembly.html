Human-robot collaborative assembly with an ABB IRB 120

A low-cost human-robot collaboration cell for industrial assembly: the robot finds and handles parts while a person works next to it, and re-plans around them.

  • WhereRobotics Lab, Ain Shams University, Cairo
  • RobotABB IRB 120 with a custom end effector
  • Perception2 × Intel RealSense D435i · YOLOv7
  • GradeA+
Demo video

Overview

The goal was a human-robot collaboration cell that is affordable to build and safe to share: the robot detects and handles the parts of an assembly such as a gearbox, while a person works in the same space.

How it works

  • Robot control: the ABB IRB 120 is driven from ROS through its EGM and RWS interfaces, with MoveIt for trajectory planning and a custom end effector for part handling.
  • Perception: two Intel RealSense D435i cameras. A custom YOLOv7 detector recognizes gears, bearings, bolts, nuts, flanges and springs (92.6% mAP@0.5), and circle and Hough-line detection align the parts.
  • Safety: MediaPipe human-pose estimation, plus YOLOv8 segmentation of people and obstacles mapped onto the RealSense depth image into an OctoMap, so MoveIt re-plans around them.
  • Interaction: voice commands (Pocketsphinx) to start and stop the robot, and a Tkinter GUI for monitoring and control.
